# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I check the status of my order?**
A: Log in and go to "My Orders," or ask the support chat for your order number (e.g., "Where is order ORD-1001?").

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Only while the order status is PENDING. Once it moves to PROCESSING or later, the address cannot be changed — see Shipping Policy.

**Q: Do you offer international shipping?**
A: We currently ship only within the United States and Canada — see Shipping Policy.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept major credit/debit cards and popular digital wallets at checkout.

**Q: How do I return an item?**
A: See Refund Policy for eligibility and the return process. You have 30 days from delivery.

**Q: My product stopped working. What do I do?**
A: Check the Warranty Policy — most products include a 1-year limited warranty. Contact support with your order number to file a claim.

**Q: How do I talk to a real person?**
A: Ask the support chat to connect you with a human, or say "I want to speak to a human," and we'll escalate your conversation to our support team.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Orders can be cancelled only while status is PENDING. After that, please wait for delivery and start a return instead.

**Q: How do I create a support ticket?**
A: You can ask the support chat to open a ticket for you, or create one manually from the Tickets page.

**Q: Is my personal data safe?**
A: Yes. See Account Policy for details on how we handle and protect your data.
