# Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty

All products purchased directly from us include a **1-year limited warranty** from the date of delivery, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## What is covered

- Defects that occur under normal use.
- Electronic component failures not caused by physical damage.
- Malfunctions present at the time of delivery.

## What is NOT covered

- Damage from misuse, drops, water exposure, or unauthorized repairs.
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., battery capacity loss after extended use).
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality.
- Products purchased from unauthorized third-party resellers.

## Filing a warranty claim

1. Contact support with your order number and a description of the issue.
2. Support may request photos or a short video showing the defect.
3. Approved claims are resolved via free repair, replacement, or store credit, at our discretion.

## Extended warranty

An optional 2-year extended warranty can be purchased at checkout for eligible products, extending coverage to a total of 3 years from delivery.

## Warranty and refunds

Filing a warranty claim does not affect your standard 30-day refund eligibility (see Refund Policy); customers may choose either remedy within the applicable window.
