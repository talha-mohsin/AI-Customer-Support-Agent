# Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, here's how refunds work.

## Eligibility

- Items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with proof of purchase (order number).
- Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.
- Items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or refund.

## How to request a refund

1. Contact support with your order number and reason for the return.
2. We will email you a prepaid return shipping label.
3. Once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your refund.

## Refund timing

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Processing takes **5-7 business days** after we receive the returned item.
- Your bank may take an additional 2-5 business days to post the refund.

## Damaged or defective items

If your item arrived damaged or defective, contact support within **7 days** of delivery for a free replacement or full refund — no return shipping required.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds may be issued if:

- The item is returned with signs of use or missing original packaging.
- Only part of a multi-item order is being returned.
