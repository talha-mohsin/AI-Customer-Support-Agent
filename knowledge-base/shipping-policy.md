# Shipping Policy

## Shipping methods and timelines

| Method          | Delivery time      | Cost               |
|------------------|--------------------|---------------------|
| Standard Shipping | 5-7 business days  | Free on orders $50+, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express Shipping  | 2-3 business days  | $12.99 |
| Overnight Shipping | 1 business day    | $24.99 |

Orders placed before 1 PM ET on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 1 PM ET, or on weekends/holidays, ship the next business day.

## Order tracking

- Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email.
- You can view tracking status and order status (PENDING, PROCESSING, SHIPPED, DELIVERED, CANCELLED) in your account under "My Orders."

## Shipping destinations

- We currently ship within the United States and Canada.
- International shipping outside the US/Canada is not currently supported.

## Delayed or lost packages

- If a package hasn't moved in tracking for more than 5 business days, contact support to open an investigation with the carrier.
- If a package is confirmed lost in transit, we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund.

## Order changes and cancellations

- Orders can be changed or cancelled only while status is **PENDING**.
- Once an order moves to **PROCESSING** or later, it cannot be modified or cancelled — you may request a return after delivery instead.
