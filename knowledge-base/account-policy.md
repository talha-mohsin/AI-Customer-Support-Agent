# Account Policy

## Creating an account

- Sign up with your name, email, and a password (minimum 6 characters).
- Every account is created with the **CUSTOMER** role by default. Support agent accounts are provisioned internally and cannot be self-registered.

## Logging in

- Accounts are authenticated with email and password using a JSON Web Token (JWT).
- If you forget your password, contact support to have it reset — self-service password reset is not currently available.

## Updating account information

- You can view your name, email, and role on your Profile page.
- To change your name or email, contact support with your current account email for verification.

## Data privacy

- We only use your account data to fulfill orders and provide support.
- We never share your personal information, order history, or conversation history with third parties without consent.
- You may request account deletion by contacting support; this permanently removes your personal data except records we are legally required to retain (such as order/financial history).

## Account security

- Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (bcrypt) and are never visible to support staff.
- If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, contact support immediately so we can secure it.
